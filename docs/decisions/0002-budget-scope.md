# ADR 0002 — 50,000 SEK Budget Scope

**Date:** Phase 1 kickoff
**Status:** Accepted (recorded for traceability)

## Decision

The 50,000 SEK planning figure in the v2 spec covers **infrastructure, licenses and legal/accounting costs only**. Development effort is **not** included.

## Implications

- Hosting, S3, monitoring, Stripe fees, domain, legal review of seller terms, accountant review of commission VAT — all draw from this budget.
- Engineering effort is accounted for separately.
- Phase 8 (real money movement) remains gated on Stripe + legal/accounting confirmation, independent of budget.
